### TRICK 01: The Bassline Handover (Low-End Swap on Beat 1)
* **Category:** Core Harmonic Blending  
* **Difficulty:** Beginner / Foundation  
* **Hardware Controls:** Deck 1 & Deck 2 Channel Low EQ Knobs, High-Pass Filter  
* **Video Reference:** [Crossfader: How to EQ Mix Like a Pro](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3Z4X8iR9_M) | [Club Ready DJ School: The Perfect Bass Swap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9j0p2b4vP3k)

#### Library Track Pairing:
* **Deck 1 (Outgoing):** `FISHER - Losing It` (127.00 BPM | Key: 6A / Gm)
* **Deck 2 (Incoming):** `Chris Lake, Skrillex - LA NOCHE` (127.00 BPM | Key: 6A / Gm)

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Set Up:** Cue Deck 2 at the start of its 16-bar intro groove. Ensure Deck 2 Low EQ is turned completely counter-clockwise to `0%` (Cut).
2. **Phrase Match:** Start Deck 2 on Bar 17 of Deck 1’s breakdown or chorus. Deck 1 and Deck 2 are running simultaneously with mid and high frequencies balanced.
3. **The Swap:** On the final beat (Beat 4) before the incoming drop, sharply rotate Deck 1’s Low EQ from `12 o'clock` to `7 o'clock` (full kill) while simultaneously snapping Deck 2’s Low EQ to `12 o'clock` (Unity) directly on **Beat 1**.
4. **Exit:** Slowly sweep Deck 1’s High-Pass Filter knob clockwise over the next 4 bars to seamlessly dissolve Deck 1 from the mix.

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip:** Never let two sub-basses play at 100% simultaneously below 120Hz. Phase cancellation will hollow out the club PA sound system and trigger limiter compression.

#### Step-by-Step Execution:

3. **Build Overlap:** Mix Deck 2’s buildup underneath Deck 1’s second breakdown.
4. **Fader Snatch:** As Deck 2’s snare riser hits 1/16th notes, pull Deck 1’s volume fader down to `0` and boost Deck 2 to master volume right on the vocal hook drop.

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Stem Isolation:** On Deck 2, press **DRUM MUTE** and **INST MUTE** on the FLX10 hardware buttons. Deck 2 now outputs **100% naked isolated vocals** (*"I wanna make you sweat!"*).

### TRICK 07: The 128 to 140 BPM Halftime Drop-In
* **Category:** Dramatic Subgenre Switch  
* **Difficulty:** Advanced  
* **Hardware Controls:** Loop Cutter, Echo FX, Crossfader  
* **Video Reference:** [Club Ready DJ School: How to Transition from House to Trap](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QeM4PzF9t8M) | [DJ Carlo: 128 to 140 BPM Transition Hack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3gA8w1vF0oM)

#### Library Track Pairing:
* **Deck 1 (Outgoing 128 BPM):** `Chris Lorenzo - In This Bih'` (126–128 BPM | Speed House)
* **Deck 2 (Incoming 140 BPM):** `ISOxo - dontstopme!` (140.00 BPM | Hybrid Trap)

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Loop Engagement:** Catch a 2-bar drum loop on Deck 1 during the outro or bridge.
2. **Loop Halving Buildup:** Halve the loop from 2 bars ➔ 1 bar ➔ 1/2 bar ➔ 1/4 bar stutter.
3. **Echo Freeze:** On the final 1/4 beat stutter, press Deck 1’s **Beat FX (3/4 Beat Delay)** and pull Deck 1’s channel fader to `0`. The echo tail repeats in 3/4 triplet time.
4. **Halftime Perception:** Fire Deck 2 (*dontstopme!*) directly on Beat 1. Because Hybrid Trap has a half-time snare on Beat 3 (70 BPM feel), the abrupt jump from 128 BPM to 140 BPM sounds massive and exhilarating rather than rushed!

#### Step-by-Step Execution:

3. **Backspin Sweep:** On beat 4 of bar 4, perform a rapid, sharp 1/2-revolution backspin on Deck 1’s jog wheel.
4. **Flowdan Entrance:** Release Deck 2 immediately on Flowdan’s vocal lead-in (*"Baddadan, baddadan, baddadan..."*). The room shifts instantaneously from sunny house groove into a subterranean UK drum & bass roller.

### TRICK 10: The "Baby Scratch" Punctuation into Beat 1
* **Category:** Classic Turntablist Drop In  
* **Difficulty:** Beginner / Intermediate  
* **Hardware Controls:** Magvel Crossfader, Jog Wheel Touch Top  
* **Scratch Tool:** `SCRATCH_VOL_01_UNIVERSAL_BATTLE_BREAKS` (Hot Cue A: *Classic Ahhh*)  
* **Video Reference:** [DJ Craze: How to Baby Scratch Like a World Champion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5zM2Y7xV8k0) | [DJ Carlo: 3 Basic Scratches Every DJ Must Know](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wX5N2rF1v_w)

#### Library Track Pairing:
* **Deck 3 (Scratch Source):** `SCRATCH_VOL_01_UNIVERSAL_BATTLE_BREAKS` (Loaded at Cue A)
* **Deck 1 (Playing Track):** `Habstrakt - Movie` (128.00 BPM)
* **Deck 2 (Incoming Track):** `Knock2 - dashstar*` (126.00 BPM)

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Buildup Breakdown:** At Bar 16 of Deck 1’s buildup, cut Deck 1’s volume fader on Beat 3.
2. **Two-Hand Coordination:**
   * Hand 1 (Right): Place hand on Deck 3’s jog wheel.
   * Hand 2 (Left): Crossfader open to Deck 3.
3. **The Cut:** Rhythmical baby scratches on Beat 3 and Beat 4:  
   `[Forward: Ahhh] ➔ [Back: hhhA] ➔ [Forward: Ahhh] ➔ [Back: hhhA]`
4. **The Release:** On the downbeat of Beat 1, snap the crossfader completely across to Deck 2 as you drop `Knock2 - dashstar*`.

### TRICK 11: The 2-Click Flare Orbit Fill
* **Category:** High-Speed Battle Cutting  
* **Difficulty:** Advanced / Master  
* **Hardware Controls:** Magvel Crossfader (2 Clicks per stroke), Jog Wheel  
* **Scratch Tool:** `SCRATCH_VOL_02_SPEED_HOUSE_CUTS` (Hot Cue B: *UK Bassline Warp in C*)  
* **Video Reference:** [Studio Scratches: How to 2-Click Flare](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0u7V8r7YxY) | [A-Trak: Scratch Practice Routine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jW9j8_w1l3E)

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **The Math of the 2-Click Flare:** You push the jog wheel forward once, but click the crossfader twice, cutting the sound into 3 pieces. On the drag back, click twice again. One forward-back hand movement produces **6 distinct notes**.
2. **Bar Placement:** Execute this in the 1-bar silence right before a heavy dubstep drop (`Ray Volpe - Laserbeam`).
3. **Sonics:** Because `SCRATCH_VOL_02` has the 120Hz high-pass filter and mid-range boost, the 6-note flare will slice cleanly through the sound system without clipping.

### TRICK 12: The Hydroplane Pitch Drag
* **Category:** Vocal / Synth Manipulation  
* **Difficulty:** Intermediate  
* **Hardware Controls:** Jog Wheel Outer Ring, Center Display  
* **Scratch Tool:** `SCRATCH_VOL_03_HYBRID_TRAP_808_SLIDES` (Hot Cue B: *Trap 808 Glide in C*)  
* **Video Reference:** [DJ Angelo: Turntable Hydroplane Technique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgV4N2mK7kU)

#### Step-by-Step Execution:
1. **Lick Your Finger:** Wet the tip of your middle finger lightly to create smooth friction against the acrylic jog-wheel top.
2. **Sustained Tone:** Hold down Hot Cue B for the sustained 808 bass tone.
3. **Drag Back:** Drag your finger backwards across the rotating jog wheel with light pressure, causing the jog wheel to shudder and bend the pitch downwards smoothly.
4. **Release:** Release right as the kick drum hits on Deck 1.
