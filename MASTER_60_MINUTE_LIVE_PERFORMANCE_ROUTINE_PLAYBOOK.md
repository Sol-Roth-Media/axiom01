## Act 1: The Induction (00:00 – 12:00) | 126.00 BPM
**Goal:** Establish immediate physical presence, calibrate sound system low-end, lock the dancefloor into a continuous 4/4 groove, and set the visual stage prompter tone.

### Routine Blueprint:
1. **Track 1 (Deck 1) – Opening Weapon:**
   * **Track:** *FISHER x Aatig – Take It Off* [BPM: 126 | Key: 8A | Energy: 4/5]
   * **Stage Action:** 
     * Start prompter HUD on iPad (`http://localhost:7777`). Prompter displays: `─── INTRO ─── [BARS: 16]`.
     * Engage FLX10 Deck 1. Filter high-pass at 12 o'clock, slowly sweep to open over 8 bars.
     * At `00:30.00` hit **Pad B (Drop 1 / Impact)**. Bass drops.
2. **Track 2 (Deck 2) – Energy Modulation (+1 Camelot):**
   * **Track:** *Chris Lake & Aluna – More Baby* [BPM: 126 | Key: 9A | Energy: 5/5]
   * **Transition Technique:** **The Bassline Handover (Technique 01)**
     * At bar 32 of Track 1, start Track 2 on **Pad A (Intro)** with Low EQ at 0.
     * Beatmatch locked on FLX10. As Track 2 reaches its pre-drop riser, sweep Track 1 Low EQ to 9 o'clock.
     * **On the downbeat of Drop 1:** Instantly cut Track 1 channel fader, drop Track 2 Low EQ to 12 o'clock.
3. **Deck 3 Accent – Alesis Drumstick Intro:**
   * **Alesis Pad 1 & 2:** Trigger *Bank 15 - Live Drum Fills & Snare Rolls* on beat 4 of every 8-bar phrase.
   * **Mic Hype:** *"We are live with physical hands tonight! Sol Roth in the booth!"*

## Act 4: The Hybrid Drumstick Assault (36:00 – 48:00) | 140 ➔ 150 BPM
**Goal:** Step out from behind the platters. Use physical acoustic drumsticks on the Alesis Strike / SamplePad to perform a live acoustic/electronic percussion solo over bass music breakdowns.

### Routine Blueprint:
1. **Track (Deck 1):**
   * **Track:** *Ray Volpe – Laserbeam* or *Subtronics – Scream Saver* [BPM: 145–150 | Key: 2A]
   * Play through Drop 1. As the track enters the melodic Breakdown (**Pad C**):
     * Loop an 8-bar atmospheric section using FLX10 **Auto Beat Loop (8 bars)**.
     * Lower Master Vault music volume by 3 dB on Channel 1 trim.
2. **Alesis Multipad Live Solo:**
   * Pick up Vic Firth 5A wooden drumsticks.
   * **Multipad Bank Selection:** *09 ALESIS SAMPLEPAD PRO / KITS / Bank 15 - Live Drum Fills & Snare Rolls*.
   * **Drumming Pattern:**
     * *Bars 1–4:* Start with 8th-note ghost snare hits on Pad 1, accenting the 2 and 4 on Pad 2 (Industrial Anvil).
     * *Bars 5–6:* Transition into 16th-note double-stroke roll across Pad 1 and Pad 3, building velocity.
     * *Bars 7–8:* Execute continuous 32nd-note buzz roll building to a peak volume crescendo.
     * *Final beat:* Hit Alesis Crash Cymbal choke on Pad 4.
3. **Drop Re-Engagement:**
   * Drop sticks into quiver, hit **Pad B (Drop 2 / Climax)** on Deck 1 with left hand.
   * Full sound system detonates.
